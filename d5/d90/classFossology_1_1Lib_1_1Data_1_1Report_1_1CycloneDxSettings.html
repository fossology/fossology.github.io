Detailed Description

CycloneDX export settings of a user (defaults) or of an upload.

users.cyclonedx_settings holds the CSV "osselot,comment,ignore[,namespace,specVersion]" (the last two are optional for rows written before they existed); report_info.ri_cyclonedx_selection holds the JSON produced by toReportInfo().
